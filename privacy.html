MIMORA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Summary

MIMORA is a private diary app. We do not operate servers that collect your diary content, and we do not sell or share your data. Your writing stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to create an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive account.

Information stored on your device

MIMORA stores the following locally on your device:

  • Diary entries (title, body, date, favorites)
  • Tasks and reminders you create
  • Photos and voice notes you attach
  • Preferences such as theme, app-lock settings, and the last backup time
  • A 24-word recovery phrase, generated on your device, and key material held in the Android Keystore

Uninstalling MIMORA or clearing its app data removes this local information.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google and choose to back up, MIMORA:

  • Encrypts a single archive on your device using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your 24-word phrase.
  • Uploads that ciphertext only to a hidden appDataFolder in your own Google Drive account.
  • Requests only the scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email.
  • Cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • Keeps the Google account email only to display which account is connected. Access tokens are short-lived and are not persisted.

Your 24-word recovery phrase is never uploaded to Google, MIMORA, or any third party. MIMORA has no servers and no recovery service: if you lose the phrase, an encrypted backup cannot be decrypted.

You can disconnect MIMORA from Google at any time in the app's Settings screen or in your Google account's connected-apps page. Disconnecting does not automatically delete an existing archive; the app's restore/delete controls and Google's permission revocation govern it.

Information we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, or crash-reporting services
  • No contacts, location, call logs, or SMS
  • No diary content transmitted to MIMORA's developers
  • No third-party sharing or sale of your information

Security

Local key material is protected with the Android Keystore. Backups use AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations) and a random salt and IV. App lock with PIN or fingerprint and a confirmation gate for destructive actions are available in Settings.

Children

MIMORA is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the MIMORA GitHub repository.
