MIMORA

A serene, private sanctuary for your daily thoughts and memories.

Write freely. Stay private.

MIMORA is an offline-first diary for Android. Your entries, photos, and voice notes live on your device. If you choose to back them up, MIMORA encrypts them on your phone before placing a single archive in a hidden folder inside your own Google Drive account.

  • Five writing canvas colors and a calm, chat-style writing page
  • Autosave with no account required
  • Optional end-to-end encrypted Google Drive backup and restore
  • Fingerprint / PIN app lock and protected deletes
  • Your 24-word private key is the only way to decrypt a backup

How Google Drive backup works

MIMORA requests only drive.appdata and userinfo.email. It can read and write files only in its own hidden appDataFolder; it cannot see your other Drive files. Google stores opaque ciphertext — MIMORA has no servers and no way to read your writing.

Anyone with a Google account can sign in. Your 24-word recovery phrase is never sent to Google or to MIMORA's developers.
